# SOP: Reviewing an ad

**Who:** the reviewer chosen on submit (normally the client's ads manager), or any owner/admin. **When:** same working day the ad is submitted.

## Steps

1. Open **Ad approval**. The **Needs me** view lists ads waiting for your review (and your own ads that need changes).
2. Open the ad. Read the creative, look at every image and video, and read the lead form.
3. Look at the **Mistake check** panel: what it found, and which issues the submitter marked Fixed or Ignore. If it was submitted with issues still open, the panel and the History say so. The checklist shows the AI's opinion next to the items it can judge ("AI: looks right", "check this", "not sure").
4. Work through the **checklist** (11 points from Retool: logo, address and phone, spelling, CTA matches headline and copy, alignment, city, tracking, naming, lead forms, Trent informed of new lead forms). Tick each one you've checked.
5. Leave **comments** for anything that needs changing. Be specific ("Headline says Tuesday, image says Thursday").
6. Decide:
   - **Request changes**: goes back to the submitter with your comments.
   - **Approve**: the submitter builds it in Meta.
7. After it's built in Meta, the submitter (or you) clicks **Mark as live**.

## Rules

- Don't approve with unticked checklist items unless the item doesn't apply (for example no new lead form). Say so in a comment.
- The AI check doesn't replace the checklist.
