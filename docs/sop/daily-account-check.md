# SOP: Daily account check

**Who:** every ads manager. **When:** every working morning, before client calls. **Time:** 10 to 15 minutes.

Replaces the daily look at Retool's Meta Ads Insights.

## Steps

Start with **Today** (the home page): it lists only what needs doing. This check is for a deeper look.

1. Open **Insights**. It starts on **your** accounts (from GHL "Assigned to") and **Last 7 days**. Data updates on its own every 30 minutes; there is no refresh button to press.
2. Check the time under the title ("Data updated 12 min ago"). If it says more than 1 hour, see [troubleshooting.md](troubleshooting.md#data-looks-old).
3. Read the **KPI tiles** at the top (active accounts, spend, leads, CPL, CPM, CR, CPR, CPLC, value generated). The small arrows compare with the previous period of the same length.
4. On the **Ad accounts** tab, use the **Sort** menu (or click a column heading): **Active CPL: high to low** first. **Active CPL** (green) counts only the ads running now; **CPL** (light blue) counts every ad that spent in the period, including ads since turned off. "No leads" in Active CPL means running ads are spending without leads: look at those first.
5. Tick any account that looks off. The **Campaigns**, **Ad sets** and **Ads** tabs then show only the ticked accounts. On the Ads tab, click an ad's picture to see its creative and copy. Each ad shows what it says (Free trial · Kids · Place callout...), and **What the ad says** filters narrow the list, e.g. Offer "Free trial" + Sort "CPL: high to low" finds the weakest free-trial ad. Press **Clear** when done: filters are remembered.
6. Check account status: anything not **Active** (for example **Unsettled** or **Disabled**) means billing or a Meta problem. Tell the client or Jordan the same day.
7. Owners and admins: to pause or stop a client, tap its status badge (Active, New...) on the Ad accounts tab, or use the **Clients** tab ([new-client-or-ad-account.md](new-client-or-ad-account.md#changing-a-client-in-the-lab)).
8. Once a week, open **What's working** for cost per lead by offer, audience, opening hook and image vs video across all clients ([../creative-tags.md](../creative-tags.md)). Rows marked "(few ads)" are hints, not results.
9. Work through **Today** ([acting-on-suggestions.md](acting-on-suggestions.md)). Most days, Today alone is enough: open Insights only for accounts Today flags or when you want the full picture.

## Good to know

- Totals are Meta's numbers. Booked, shown, closed and value come from the client's lead sheet (copied from Retool every 15 minutes).
- Money is shown in each account's own currency. Totals across USD and CAD accounts are added as-is, the same as Retool did.
- To see another ads manager's accounts (cover, holidays), change the ads manager filter (top right), or pick **All ads managers**.
- **CSV** downloads exactly what the current tab shows.
