# SOP: Creating ads with a style (Ad Studio)

**Who:** whoever builds the ad. **When:** instead of making the image in a Claude chat, for any style that's in Ad Studio (Echo for now). **Time:** 2 to 5 minutes.

How it works behind the scenes: [../ad-studio.md](../ad-studio.md). Nothing is generated by AI: the MRKD style kit's own code draws the ad in your browser, with the kit's approved people.

## Steps

1. Open the ad in **Ad approval** (or create it: **New ad**, pick the client).
2. In **Images and videos**, click **Create with a style**.
3. **Gym:** check it says **Gracie Barra** for GB locations and **Generic gym** for everyone else. GB people wear GB patches; The Lab only offers them for Gracie Barra.
4. **Start from:** pick the closest example (free trial, rest of the year, kickboxing, or Black Friday). The client's name, city and a short code fill in.
5. **Words on the ad:** change every line to this client's **real** offer. Lines still copied from the example are flagged "From the example". Keep the two big lines short (FREE / TRIAL, SAVE / $300).
6. Tick **"I've checked every line matches this client's real offer"**. Ads can't be made without it. Black Friday wording also needs its own tick, and only for a real Black Friday offer.
7. **Ads:** for each one, pick a person, colours (cream, charcoal, navy, red), colour or black-and-white photo, and person left or right. Up to 6.
8. Click **Make ads**. The first time on a device it takes a little longer (it downloads the design engine once).
9. Look at each ad **at full size**: faces not under the words, spelling, dates and prices right. Tap an ad to un-pick it.
10. Click **Add to this ad**. Then carry on with the copy and **Submit** as usual ([ad-approval-submit.md](ad-approval-submit.md)).

## Good to know

- Want a style that isn't there yet, a client's own photo, or a new person? Use the Claude chat with the MRKD ad styles skill as before, and ask for the style to be added to Ad Studio.
- Black Friday Canva template (BF) stays in Canva: paste the Canva link in the ad.
- "Couldn't load the style kit": reload; still failing, see [troubleshooting.md](troubleshooting.md#ad-studio-wont-load).

## Adding a style (owners, with Claude)

1. Ask Claude to add the style by its code (for example COMIC). Claude reads its BUILD.md, loads its zips from the Drive kit with `scripts/style-kit/load_style.py`, adds its rules to `shared/domain/styleKit.ts` and its card to Ad Studio, tests a render, and updates [../ad-studio.md](../ad-studio.md).
2. Files over 10 MB can't come through the Drive connector: attach those zips to the Claude session instead.
3. Retired styles (SP, PC, 3S, INK, TEEN) are never added.
