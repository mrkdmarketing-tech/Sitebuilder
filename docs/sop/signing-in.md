# SOP: Signing in

**Who:** every staff member. **When:** first time, new device, or when signed out.

## Steps

1. Open **https://sitebuilder-6iw.pages.dev**. On a phone, add it to the home screen (Safari: Share, Add to Home Screen; Chrome: menu, Add to Home screen).
2. Choose one:
   - **Continue with Google** and pick your **@mrkd.ca** account (available once Google sign-in is switched on, see [../setup/hosting-and-signin.md](../setup/hosting-and-signin.md)).
   - **Email me a sign-in link**: type your @mrkd.ca email, open the email on the same device, tap the link.
3. **Owners and admins** (and anyone who turned it on): enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. The first time, you'll be asked to set it up: scan the QR code with Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy or 1Password, then type the code ([security.md](security.md#two-step-sign-in-in-the-lab)).
4. You land on **Insights**, already filtered to the accounts assigned to you in GHL.

You stay signed in. To sign out, click your initials in the top right.

## If something goes wrong

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| "Not on the MRKD staff list" | Your email isn't on your staff record. Ask an admin to follow [staff-and-access.md](staff-and-access.md). |
| No sign-in email | Check spam. The backup email sender only allows a few emails per hour across MRKD; wait 10 minutes or use Google. |
| The link opens but you're not signed in | Open the link on the **same device and browser** you requested it from. |
| "The Lab was updated. Reload" | Click **Reload**. You were on an older version. |
| The code doesn't work | Codes change every 30 seconds; use the current one. Check your phone's time is set automatically. |
| Lost or new phone | New phone: Settings → Two-step sign-in → Move to a new phone. Lost: ask an owner to **Reset two-step** in Settings → Staff ([security.md](security.md#two-step-sign-in-in-the-lab)). |
